## The line (111 characters)

> You should diversify your savings: half in one meme coin, half in the same meme coin after it changes its name.

## Why this line

The advice wears the costume of sound financial guidance — "diversify your savings" — then reveals the diversification is an illusion: half in one meme coin, half in the exact same meme coin after a rebrand. The twist lands in the final clause, where a name change is treated as a different asset.
